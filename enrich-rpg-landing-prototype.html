The journey

How the quest begins

Three steps stand between you and the hero you want to become.

01
Choose your track

Pick any area of your life you want to level up — Health, Social, Finance, Learning, Creativity. Each becomes its own epic quest line with its own star counter.

02
Complete quests, earn stars

Every task you complete earns stars. Hit milestones at 1, 10, 25, 50, and 100 stars — and keep earning bonus stars beyond that, every 20 stars forever.

03
Claim your real-world rewards

You define the rewards. A cheat day at 25 stars. A solo day out at 50. A vanity treat at 10. Real-world incentives that keep you playing the long game.
